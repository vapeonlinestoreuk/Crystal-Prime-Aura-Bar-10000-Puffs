How to Choose Your Perfect PIXL Flavour

With more than 60 PIXL flavours across both devices, choosing can feel tricky. These simple tips will help you narrow it down:

  • Start with what you already like. If you enjoy fruit sweets, try Starmix or Rainbow Rush. If you prefer fresh, cool flavours, start with Fresh Mint or Cherry Ice.
  • Think about all-day vaping. Very sweet flavours can feel heavy after a few hours. Many vapers keep a sweet flavour for the evening and a lighter citrus or mint flavour for the daytime.
  • Try an ice flavour if you find fruit too sweet. The cooling finish on flavours such as Mango Ice and Pineapple Ice balances the sweetness.
  • Use the Duo 12 to test two flavours at once. Each Duo 12 edition gives you two flavours in one device, which makes it an easy way to try something new without committing to a full pod.
  • Mix and match with the multibuy. Because every Duo 12 edition is the same price, the 3 for £15 deal lets you try three different flavours for £5 each.

Tips to Get the Best Flavour From PIXL Pods

  • Top up from the refill container. PIXL 8000 pods come with a 10ml refill container, and Duo 12 packs include two 5ml containers. Top up before the pod runs dry to avoid a burnt taste.
  • Replace the pod when the flavour fades. When the vapour drops or the taste gets weaker, it is time for a fresh pod.
  • Keep the battery charged. Both kits charge with USB-C. A low battery can make the flavour feel weaker.
  • Make sure the pod is seated properly. Duo 12 pods click into place with a magnetic connection. A loose pod can affect performance.
