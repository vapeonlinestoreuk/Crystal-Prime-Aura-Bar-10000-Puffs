PIXL 8000 or Duo 12: Which Is Better for Flavour?

Honestly, it comes down to how you vape.

If you've got one flavour you love and you don't want to think about it, get the PIXL 8000. You get more choice, more puffs per pod and one flavour that stays the same from start to finish.

If you get bored easily, the Duo 12 is the better fit. Two flavours in one device means you can switch from something sweet to something fresh without changing anything. It's also slightly cheaper if you use the 3 for £15 offer.

How to Pick the Right PIXL Flavour for You

A few things we tell customers who can't decide:

  • Go with what you already like. If you love fruit sweets, Starmix or Rainbow Rush will feel familiar. If you prefer something clean, start with Fresh Mint.
  • Think about the whole day. Very sweet flavours can get a bit much by the afternoon. Plenty of our regulars keep one sweet flavour and one citrus or mint on the go.
  • Find fruit too sweet? Try an ice version. The cool finish on Mango Ice or Pineapple Ice takes the edge off.
  • Use the Duo 12 to experiment. Two flavours per device is the cheapest way to try something new without committing to a full pod.

Getting the Best Taste From Your PIXL Pods

A great flavour can still taste flat if the pod isn't looked after. Four quick habits help:

  • Top up before it runs dry. Both pods come with refill containers (10ml for the 8000, two 5ml for the Duo 12). Letting the pod empty completely is the quickest way to a burnt taste.
  • Change the pod when the flavour fades. If the vapour gets thin or the taste goes dull, the pod's done.
  • Keep the battery charged. Both kits use USB-C. A flat battery can make any flavour feel weaker.
  • Make sure the pod is sitting properly. The Duo 12 pods click in with a magnet. If one feels loose, take it out and refit it.
