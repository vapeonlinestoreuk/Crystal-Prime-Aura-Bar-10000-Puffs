TL;DR: Smok TFV12 coils are the replacement heads for the original Smok TFV12 Cloud Beast King tank: a pack of 3 at £9.95 (was £11.95) in 0.12, 0.15 and 0.17 ohm. Best for direct-to-lung (DTL) vapers still running this 2016 tank on a 200W-plus dual-battery mod. Not for the Smok TFV12 Prince or for mouth-to-lung vaping.

Smok's V12 family: 0.12 ohm is the twelve-coil V12-T12, 0.15 ohm a quad-coil V12-X4 or V12-Q4, and 0.17 ohm the six-coil V12-T6. All are sub-ohm (under 1 ohm) high-wattage coils from the Smok coils range. This is the Cloud Beast King coil, not the Prince coil.

Key Facts

  • Smok TFV12 coils fit the Smok TFV12 Cloud Beast King tank only.
  • The Smok V12-T12 0.12 ohm coil fires up to 350W, best at 130 to 200W.
  • Smok TFV12 coils do not fit the Smok TFV12 Prince tank.

Smok TFV12 Coils: Fits and Does Not Fit

Fits the Cloud Beast King tank only. Does not fit:

  • Smok TFV12 Prince and P-Tank: take Smok TFV12 Prince coils
  • Smok TFV12 Baby Prince, TFV8 Baby and Big Baby: V8 Baby coils
  • Smok TFV8 Cloud Beast: V8 coils
  • Smok TFV16 and TFV16 Lite: V16 coils
  • Smok TFV18 and TFV18 Mini: V18 coils

Which Smok TFV12 Coil to Pick

  • 0.12 ohm (V12-T12): twelve coils, rated to 350W, best at 130 to 200W. Biggest clouds, thirstiest on juice and batteries.
  • 0.15 ohm (V12-X4 or V12-Q4): quad coil, easiest on batteries and juice. Listed at 90 to 160W (Q4) or up to 220W (X4).
  • 0.17 ohm (V12-T6): six coils, 90 to 320W, best at 120 to 180W. More flavour-led than the T12. Most listings print this head as 0.16 ohm; trust the figure on your coil.

Which Mod Can Drive These Coils

Use a dual 18650 or 21700 mod of 200W or more. The Smok Scar 18 kit fires to 230W and covers every best range here; see the vape mods range for other 200W options. Batteries are sold separately.

Best E-Liquid for These Coils

Use a thick, high-VG 100ml shortfill e-liquid at 70/30 or 80/20 with a nicotine shot for 3mg, or 6mg at most. Never use nic salts or 50/50: thin liquid floods and leaks, and 20mg at 150W is far too harsh.

How to Fit and Prime

  1. Unscrew the base from the glass and remove the old coil.
  2. Screw the new coil in firmly, not forced.
  3. Drip e-liquid into each juice port until the cotton is wet.
  4. Refit, fill and wait at least 10 minutes.
  5. Start at 90W (T6) or 130W (T12) and raise in 10W steps.

How Long They Last and When to Change

Expect one to two weeks per coil on a clear 3mg shortfill; sweet liquids and chain vaping above 180W can halve that. Change it when flavour dulls, hits taste burnt or the tank gurgles.
