How much are Vaporesso pods?

How many puffs are in a Vaporesso pod?

A prefilled Vaporesso Dojo Blast 10k pod is rated for up to 10,000 puffs from its 10ml, and the Vaporesso Dojo Blast 30K Pro kit for up to 30,000 across two pods in ECO mode. A refillable 2ml Vaporesso XROS pod has no puff rating: it holds 2ml per fill and the coil lasts one to two weeks.

What kind of vape juice for Vaporesso?

For Vaporesso XROS, Vaporesso Eco Nano and Vaporesso Luxe X pods at 0.6Ω and above, a 50/50 nic salt such as Vaporesso Dojo Liq or Deliciu at 10mg or 20mg. For the 0.3Ω and 0.4Ω Vaporesso Luxe X pods and any Vaporesso iTank on Vaporesso GTi coils, a high-VG shortfill at 3mg to 6mg. Thick liquid wicks badly in a 1.2Ω pod.

How do you refill a Vaporesso pod?

Vaporesso XROS pods fill from the top: pull the mouthpiece off, fill the port beside the chimney to just below the line, refit the mouthpiece and wait five minutes on a new pod. Vaporesso Luxe X and Vaporesso Eco Nano pods fill from the bottom through a silicone stopper. Vaporesso Dojo Blast pods are prefilled and feed from the attached container.

Why does my Vaporesso taste burnt?

Usually a new pod fired before the cotton soaked, a pod run dry, or a liquid too thick for the coil. Fill a fresh pod, leave it five minutes and keep the level above the window. On a Vaporesso XROS 5 Mini or Vaporesso XROS Pro 2, match the wattage to the rating printed on the pod, such as 16W for 0.8Ω.

Why is my Vaporesso not working?

Check three things. The pod: pull it out and reseat it, and on a Vaporesso Dojo Blast kit make sure both silicone plugs are removed. The lock: button kits such as the Vaporesso XROS 5 Mini lock with five quick presses. The charge: a flat 1000mAh Vaporesso XROS takes about an hour on a 5V/1A charger.
