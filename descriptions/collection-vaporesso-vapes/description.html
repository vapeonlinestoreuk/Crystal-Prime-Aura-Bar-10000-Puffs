Which Vaporesso Vape for MTL, RDL and DTL

Mouth to lung, the tight draw, is the XROS range on a 0.8Ω, 1.0Ω or 1.2Ω pod with a 10mg or 20mg nic salt, and every Dojo Blast prefilled kit. Restricted direct lung is the 0.6Ω XROS pod, the XROS 5 Mini and Pro 2 with adjustable wattage, the Luxe X on a 0.6Ω pod and the Eco Nano on its 0.8Ω pod. Direct lung is the Luxe X on 0.3Ω or 0.4Ω pods, or the iTank on GTi coils driven by a Gen, Armour S or Armour Max mod, on a 3mg to 6mg shortfill.

Vaporesso XROS Pods Compatibility: Which Pod Fits Which Kit

Every XROS kit uses the same magnetic pod seat, so an XROS pod from 0.6Ω up fits the XROS, XROS 2, 3, 4, 5 and 6, every Mini and Nano, the Pro and the Cube. The trap is wattage: the 0.4Ω COREX 2.0 pod fires at 25W and is listed for the XROS 4, 4 Mini, 5, 5 Mini, Pro and Pro 2 only. COREX 3.0 pods use the same seat and resistances, so the same rule applies. Luxe pods do not fit XROS kits; the Luxe XR and XR Max take replaceable GTX coils inside their pods, the Luxe X uses fixed-coil pods.

Vaporesso GTi Coils vs GT Coils and GTX Coils

Three coil families, three sockets. GTi coils are the current sub-ohm platform for the iTank, iTank 2 and iTank T, and ship with the Target and Armour kits. GT Core coils are the older NRG-era platform, packs of 3 from £5.99, for the NRG tanks and the Cascade with its adaptor; they do not fit an iTank. GTX coils are the push-in coil for the Luxe XR, XR Max, PM40, Luxe 80 S and GTX One pods, £8.95 to £9.99 for 5. iTank means GTi, NRG means GT, a coil that drops into a pod is GTX.

COREX 2.0 vs COREX 3.0 Pods

COREX is Vaporesso's name for its pod heating design. COREX 2.0 reworked the mesh and cotton in the XROS pod and arrived with the XROS 4 in 2024; COREX 3.0 swapped the flat mesh for a Hive Mesh in a new chimney and debuted on the XROS 5. Both are £2.25 per pod in a pack of 4 here. Buy 3.0 for an XROS 5, 6 or Pro 2 and 2.0 for anything older, unless you want to try the newer coil, which fits.

Which Vaporesso pod is the best?

How many puffs are in a Vaporesso pod?

A prefilled Vaporesso Dojo Blast 10k pod is rated for up to 10,000 puffs from its 10ml, and the 30K Pro kit for up to 30,000 across two pods in ECO mode. A refillable 2ml XROS pod has no puff rating: it holds 2ml per fill and the coil lasts one to two weeks.

What kind of vape juice for Vaporesso?

For XROS, Eco Nano and Luxe X pods at 0.6Ω and above, a 50/50 nic salt such as Vaporesso Dojo Liq or Deliciu at 10mg or 20mg. For the 0.3Ω and 0.4Ω Luxe X pods and any iTank on GTi coils, a high-VG shortfill at 3mg to 6mg. Thick liquid wicks badly in a 1.2Ω pod.

How do you refill a Vaporesso pod?

XROS pods fill from the top: pull the mouthpiece off, fill the port beside the chimney to just below the line, refit the mouthpiece and wait five minutes on a new pod. Luxe X and Eco Nano pods fill from the bottom through a silicone stopper. Dojo Blast pods are prefilled and feed from the attached container.

Why does my Vaporesso taste burnt?

Usually a new pod fired before the cotton soaked, a pod run dry, or a liquid too thick for the coil. Fill a fresh pod, leave it five minutes and keep the level above the window. On an XROS 5 Mini or Pro 2, match the wattage to the rating printed on the pod, such as 16W for 0.8Ω.

Why is my Vaporesso not working?

Check three things. The pod: pull it out and reseat it, and on a Dojo Blast kit make sure both silicone plugs are removed. The lock: button kits such as the XROS 5 Mini lock with five quick presses. The charge: a flat 1000mAh XROS takes about an hour on a 5V/1A charger.
