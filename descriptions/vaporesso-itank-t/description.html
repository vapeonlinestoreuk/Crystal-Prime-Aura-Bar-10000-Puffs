TL;DR: The Vaporesso iTank T is a top-airflow sub ohm tank (a direct-to-lung tank for low-resistance coils) for any 510 mod, sold here as the UK 2ml TPD version with two GTi mesh coils in the box. Best for DTL vapers; not for MTL vapers.

Vaporesso iTank T Key Facts

  • The Vaporesso iTank T is a 24.5mm sub ohm tank with a threaded 510 connection and an 810 drip tip.
  • The Vaporesso iTank T uses Vaporesso GTi press-fit mesh coils.
  • The Vaporesso iTank T ships with a GTi 0.2Ω coil (60 to 75W) and a GTi 0.4Ω coil (50 to 60W).
  • The UK TPD version of the Vaporesso iTank T holds 2ml; the international version holds 6ml.
  • The Vaporesso iTank T has an adjustable top airflow ring and a sliding top-fill cap, so it cannot drain through the airflow slots when laid on its side.

Vaporesso iTank T: Fits and Does Not Fit

The tank fits any 510 mod that can deliver 30 to 90W, such as the Vaporesso Armour S mod. It is also the stock tank of the Vaporesso Gen Max and Gen SE kits.

This is the iTank T, not the iTank 2. Both take GTi coils, but the Vaporesso iTank 2 has bottom airflow and a flip-top fill. The MTL Vaporesso iTank X (GTX coils) and Vaporesso iTank M (MTX coils) do not take GTi coils, and their coils do not fit this tank.

Which Vaporesso GTi Coil to Pick

  • GTi 0.2Ω, 60 to 75W (pre-installed): warm, open DTL draw, most vapour.
  • GTi 0.4Ω, 50 to 60W (spare in the box): cooler, tighter DTL; best on an 80W single-battery mod.
  • GTi 0.15Ω (75 to 90W) and GTi 0.5Ω (30 to 40W) also fit: 200W dual-battery mods and low-power restricted DTL respectively.

Best E-Liquid for the Vaporesso iTank T

Use a 70VG/30PG or thicker shortfill e-liquid in 0mg or 3mg freebase. Nic salts and 50/50 liquids are too thin and will flood the coil.

How to Fit and Prime a GTi Coil

  1. Unscrew the base and pull the old coil straight down; Planet of the Vapes forum members report GTi coils can split if twisted out.
  2. Wet every cotton port of the new coil, press it into the base and refill.
  3. Wait 10 minutes, then start at the bottom of the coil's wattage range.

Coil Life and Running Cost

A GTi mesh coil usually lasts one to two weeks, less on dark or sweet liquids: at the current VOS price of £9.95 for five, that is £1 to £2 a week. Change it when flavour fades or tastes burnt. See all Vaporesso tanks and DTL vape tanks.
