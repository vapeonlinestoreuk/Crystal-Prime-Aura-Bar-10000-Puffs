Nic Shot Maths: 3mg or 6mg From a 100ml Shortfill

A nicotine shot is 10ml of flavourless liquid at 18mg freebase or 20mg nic salt, and the maths is total nicotine divided by total volume. Two 18mg shots hold 360mg; add them to 100ml of Wow Exp 100ml E Liquid and you have 120ml holding 360mg, which is 3mg per ml. Two shots cannot reach 6mg: four 18mg shots in 100ml gives 140ml at just over 5mg, and a true 6mg needs four shots in an 80ml fill. So the 6mg option is a different mix from the 3mg one; check how many shots come with it before you pour. Our nicotine shots are sold separately.

How to Add Nic Shots and Make the Bottle Last

Mixing step by step

  1. Press down on the childproof cap, twist it off and pull out the narrow nozzle insert.
  2. Squeeze the full 10ml of your nicotine shot into the shortfill, and repeat with the second shot for 3mg.
  3. Push the nozzle back in, screw the cap on tightly and shake hard for at least a minute.
  4. Leave the bottle to stand overnight so the nicotine spreads evenly.
  5. Prime a new coil and wait ten minutes after the first fill before you fire it.

Making 120ml go further

  • Keep the bottle capped, upright and out of sunlight; heat and UV flatten soda flavours within weeks.
  • Drop your wattage by 5W to 10W when you want flavour rather than clouds; lower power uses less liquid per draw.
  • Change the coil when the fizz goes flat; a tired coil wastes liquid and mutes the soda top note.
