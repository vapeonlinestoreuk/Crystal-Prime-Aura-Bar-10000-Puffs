Vaporesso LUXE X Dual Mesh Pods are the twin-coil refill pods for the Vaporesso LUXE X family, two pods for £5.50. Choose 0.4Ω for a looser restricted lung draw or 0.6Ω for a tighter mouth-to-lung draw. This is the Dual Mesh pod, not the single-mesh Vaporesso LUXE X pod and not the empty Vaporesso LUXE XR pod that takes GTX coils. Best for Vaporesso LUXE X3 owners; not ideal if you want a 0.8Ω cigarette-tight draw.

Vaporesso LUXE X Dual Mesh Pods: Key Facts

  • The Vaporesso LUXE X Dual Mesh Pod is the pod supplied with the Vaporesso LUXE X3 kit.
  • Vaporesso LUXE X Dual Mesh Pods come in 0.4Ω and 0.6Ω, two pods per pack.
  • UK Vaporesso LUXE X Dual Mesh Pods hold 2ml.

Fits and Does Not Fit: Vaporesso LUXE X Pod Compatibility

The magnetic LUXE X pod base fits:

  • Vaporesso LUXE X3 (its stock pod), Vaporesso LUXE X, Vaporesso LUXE X Pro and Vaporesso LUXE X2
  • Vaporesso LUXE XR, Vaporesso LUXE XR Max and Vaporesso LUXE XR Max 2

It does not fit:

  • Vaporesso LUXE Q, LUXE Q2, LUXE Q2 SE and LUXE QS (LUXE Q pod)
  • Vaporesso LUXE PM40 and Vaporesso XROS kits (GTX and XROS pods)

Which Vaporesso LUXE X Dual Mesh Pod to Pick

  • 0.4Ω Dual Mesh: restricted direct-lung (a loose pull straight to the lungs), warmer, denser vapour. Suits 70/30 or 60/40 shortfill users.
  • 0.6Ω Dual Mesh: tighter mouth-to-lung (held in the mouth first, like a cigarette), lower power, longer pod life. Suits nic salts and ex-disposable vapers.

Key Specs: Vaporesso LUXE X Dual Mesh Pods

  • Resistance: 0.4Ω or 0.6Ω. Lower ohms give more vapour and use e-liquid faster.
  • Coil: COREX dual mesh, built in. The whole pod is replaced; no coil to swap.
  • Capacity: 2ml (UK TPD version). Vaporesso's global listing is 5ml.
  • Pack size: 2 pods. £5.50 a pack is £2.75 a pod.

Best E-Liquid for Vaporesso LUXE X Dual Mesh Pods

Run the 0.6Ω pod on 50/50 e-liquid, ideally 10mg or 20mg nic salt. Run the 0.4Ω pod on a 60/40 or 70/30 shortfill at 3mg to 6mg; thick 80/20 liquid starves either pod. Browse 10ml nic salts and vape juice.

How to Fit and Prime a Vaporesso LUXE X Dual Mesh Pod

  1. Pull the old pod straight off the magnets.
  2. Lift the silicone stopper, fill to just below the top and close it firmly.
  3. Leave the pod standing for at least 10 minutes.
  4. Seat the pod, take two or three gentle draws, then vape from the kit's lowest setting.

How Long Vaporesso LUXE X Dual Mesh Pods Last

Expect one to two weeks per pod at moderate use. Change it when flavour fades, hits taste burnt or the pod gurgles.
