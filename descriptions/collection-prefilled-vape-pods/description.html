Prefilled Pods Questions, Answered

What are pre-filled vape pods?

Pre-filled vape pods are sealed 2ml pods of nic salt e-liquid that clip into a rechargeable pod kit. The UK format pairs the 2ml pod with a 10ml refill container, so one pod runs for thousands of puffs before you swap it. Every product here is that type.

Can I refill a prefilled vape pod?

No. Prefilled pods are sealed at the factory and the 10ml container feeds the 2ml pod on its own; there is no fill port. When the liquid runs out you replace the pod. If you want to fill your own, use the refillable pods on the replacement vape pods page.

Which pre-filled pod vape is the best?

By sales, the Hayati Pro Ultra Plus 25000: 11,346 pod packs in the last four months and 121 reviews at 4.59 stars. By rating among the top five, the Crystal Pro CP 10K pod at 4.71 from 56 reviews. By price per ml, the Solo Bar 15K pod at 23p per ml.

How do I use a prefilled pod vape?

Pull the old pod out, unwrap the new one, remove the silicone bungs from the mouthpiece and base, and push it in until it clicks. Most kits are draw activated, so inhale and it fires. Charge the kit over USB-C when the light drops; the pod never needs charging.

Are prefilled vape pods being banned?

No. Prefilled pods for rechargeable kits are on sale in the UK, within the 2ml pod limit and 20mg nicotine cap. Everything here comes through authorised UK distribution.

Do Hayati pods fit a Lost Mary kit?

No, and no pod here fits a kit from another brand. Hayati Pro Ultra Plus pods fit the Hayati Pro Ultra Plus 25000 only, Lost Mary Nera 15K pods fit the Lost Mary Nera 30K only, and so on even within a brand. Use the Fits column and match the exact model name.

Are there 10mg prefilled vape pods?

Yes. IVG Pro 12 pods come in 10mg, Hayati Pro Max Plus 6000 pods have a separate 10mg listing, and Lost Mary BM6000 and Vaporesso Dojo Blast 10K pods are stocked in 10mg too. 20mg is the default on every top seller; 0mg exists for IVG Pro 12, Hayati Pro Max Plus and Lost Mary BM6000.

How long does a prefilled pod last?

A 12ml pod rated at 10,000 puffs lasts a typical pod-kit user one to two weeks; a 6,000-puff pod closer to a week. Replace it when the flavour fades or the draw turns harsh, which is the coil drying out as the container empties. The clear container on most kits shows the level.

What is a prefilled pod kit?

A prefilled pod kit is the rechargeable battery body these pods clip into. It comes with one pod in the box and is reused; the pods are the consumable. Kits are listed on the prefilled pod vape kits page, and the Fits column above names the kit each pod belongs to.
