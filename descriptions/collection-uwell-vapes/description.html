Pro-FOCS: What Uwell's Flavour Tech Does

Pro-FOCS is Uwell's name for the airflow path inside its coils, which routes the air through the coil chamber so the vapour picks up more condensed flavour before it reaches the mouthpiece. It has been on every Uwell Caliburn pod since the original 1.4Ω Uwell Caliburn, and the Uwell Caliburn GPP pods on the Uwell Caliburn G5 and G5 Lite SE carry the fourth version, Pro-FOCS 4.0. The Uwell Whirl coils and Uwell Whirl S2 pods use it too. In practice it means an Uwell pod tastes clean on the first fill and holds that taste for most of the pod's life.

MTL vs RDL on a Uwell: Match the Ohms

Mouth to lung, the tight draw, is every Uwell Caliburn A-series kit, every Uwell Viscore, the Uwell Caliburn G3 Pro and G5 on a 0.9Ω or 1.2Ω pod with the airflow closed, and the Uwell Whirl 2 on its 1.8Ω coil. Use a 10mg or 20mg nic salt. Restricted direct lung is the Uwell Caliburn G3, G3 Pro, G5 and G5 Lite SE on a 0.4Ω or 0.6Ω pod with the airflow open, and the Uwell Crown M on its 0.4Ω side, on a 50/50 or 70/30 liquid at 3mg to 6mg. Full direct lung is the Uwell Whirl 2 on 0.6Ω, the Uwell Crown V tank at £26.99 on Uwell Crown V coils, or the Uwell Crown 4 kit at £28.99, driven by a mod and a high-VG shortfill. Do not put a 20mg salt in a 0.4Ω pod; the throat hit is too much and the coil burns through liquid fast.

How Long Uwell Coils and Pods Last

Plan on one to two weeks per refillable Uwell Caliburn pod on a nic salt or an unsweetened 50/50, less on sweet dessert liquids that coat the mesh. The Uwell Caliburn GPP Alpha pod uses Uwell's AlphaCore design for a longer life than the standard Uwell Caliburn GPP pod, which is why it comes fitted in the Uwell Caliburn G5. Prime every new pod: fill it, leave it five to ten minutes, then start with short draws. Prefilled Uwell Viscore pods are rated by puffs rather than weeks: 20,000 for the Uwell Viscore Pro+ pod, 8,000 for the Uwell Viscore 8000 pod, 6,000 for the Uwell Viscore Neo 6K pod. Sub-ohm Uwell Whirl and Uwell Valyrian coils last one to three weeks depending on wattage and liquid.

What is the difference between Uwell Viscore and Uwell Caliburn?

What juice goes in a Uwell vape?

A 50/50 nic salt at 10mg or 20mg for any Uwell Caliburn A-series kit, a 0.9Ω or 1.2Ω Uwell Caliburn GPP pod, or a 1.8Ω Uwell Whirl coil. A 50/50 or 70/30 liquid at 3mg to 6mg for 0.4Ω and 0.6Ω pods and the Uwell Crown M. A high-VG shortfill for the Uwell Whirl 2 on 0.6Ω and the Uwell Crown V tank.

How do you fill a Uwell vape?

Uwell Caliburn A-series pods fill from the side: pull the silicone plug, fill to just below the top, push it back and wait five minutes. How long does a Uwell take to charge?

Why does my Uwell taste burnt?

Usually a new pod fired before the cotton soaked, a pod run dry below the window, or a thick 70/30 liquid in a 1.0Ω or 1.2Ω pod. Fit a fresh pod, leave it ten minutes, keep the liquid above the fill line, and match the liquid to the ohms as above.
