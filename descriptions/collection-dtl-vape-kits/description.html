DTL vs MTL vs RDL: What Direct to Lung Means

Direct to lung (DTL) means you breathe the vapour straight into your lungs in one motion. Mouth to lung (MTL) is the cigarette-style pull: into the mouth, then inhaled. Restricted direct lung (RDL) sits between. DTL needs a coil under 0.5 ohm, open airflow and 40W or more; MTL pod kits stop at 16 to 30W. Coming from a disposable, you have been vaping MTL: try the Mag V8 or Veynom EX before a 200W mod.

DTL Vape Coils, Ohms and Wattage

Sub ohm means under 1.0 ohm; the DTL coils here run 0.15 to 0.4 ohm, each with a wattage window printed on it: PnP-TW15 0.15 ohm at 55 to 70W, PnP-TW20 0.2 ohm at 40 to 55W, Aspire BP 0.15 ohm at 60 to 80W, BP 0.3 ohm at 30 to 40W. Lower resistance means more vapour, heat and liquid per puff. Stay inside the window: above it the cotton burns, below it the coil floods. DTL Vape Kit Questions, Answered

What is DTL in vaping?

DTL stands for direct to lung: you inhale the vapour straight into your lungs in one breath instead of holding it in your mouth first. It uses sub ohm coils below 1.0 ohm, open airflow and 40W or more, giving a warm, dense cloud and using more liquid than a pod kit.

Is it better to MTL or DTL?

Neither is better; they suit different people. MTL copies a cigarette draw, uses less liquid and works with 10mg or 20mg nic salts. DTL gives bigger vapour and fuller flavour at 3mg or 6mg but costs more in liquid and power. Most ex-disposable users start MTL.

Is sub-ohm vaping better?

Sub ohm vaping gives more vapour and a warmer, fuller flavour than a high-resistance coil, which is why DTL vapers choose it. It is not for everyone: it drains batteries faster, uses 5 to 10ml of liquid a day and needs 3mg or 6mg shortfill. For a tight, quiet draw, a 0.8 or 1.2 ohm pod kit suits you more.

Is 0.6 or 0.8 ohm better for vape?

Both sit above the DTL range on this page. A 0.6 ohm coil gives a restricted direct lung draw at 20 to 30W with more vapour; a 0.8 ohm coil gives a mouth to lung draw at 12 to 18W with less liquid use. The box mods in the first two tables want 0.15 to 0.4 ohm coils.

What juice is best for sub-ohm vaping?

A 70VG/30PG shortfill at 3mg or 6mg. The high VG gives thick vapour and does not leak through wide-wicked coils, and the low nicotine stops a lung hit being harsh. Avoid 50/50 nic salts at 10mg or 20mg in any kit above 40W. VOS 50ml shortfills start at £2.99.

How do you DTL vape?

Open the airflow fully, set the wattage inside the window printed on the coil, then breathe in slowly and directly to your lungs for two to three seconds without pausing in the mouth, and exhale straight away. Start at the bottom of the coil's range and raise it in 5W steps until the vapour is warm but not harsh.
