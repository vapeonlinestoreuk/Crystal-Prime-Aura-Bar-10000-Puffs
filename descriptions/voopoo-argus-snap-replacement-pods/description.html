Voopoo Argus Snap Replacement Pods: Introduction

Voopoo Argus Snap Replacement Pods are the clamshell top-fill cartridges for the Voopoo Argus P3 and every Argus Pod Family kit, three per pack in 0.4Ω, 0.7Ω or 1.0Ω at the price shown. This is the hinged-cap Snap pod, not the silicone-plug Voopoo Argus Top-Fill V2 pod cartridges.

Voopoo Argus Snap Pods: Key Facts

  • Voopoo Argus Snap Replacement Pods hold 2ml of e-liquid.
  • Voopoo Argus Snap Replacement Pods come in 0.4Ω, 0.7Ω and 1.0Ω with the coil built in.
  • Voopoo Argus Snap Replacement Pods use iCOSM CODE 2.0 mesh coils rated for 100ml of e-liquid.
  • Voopoo Argus Snap Replacement Pods fit every Voopoo Argus Pod Family kit, including the Voopoo Argus P3.

Voopoo Argus Snap Pods: Fits and Does Not Fit

  • Fits: Voopoo Argus P1, P1S, P2, P3, G2, G2 Mini, G3, G3 Mini, Klyc, Matrix, Pod, Pod SE, Z and Z2.
  • Check the box card: Voopoo Argus Z3, G4 and G4 Mini, which Voopoo covers only as "and more".
  • 0.4Ω pod: 30W Argus kits only, such as the Voopoo Argus P3 and G3.
  • Does not fit: Voopoo Argus Pro, Pro 2, GT, GT II, XT, MT and X (PnP pods), or the Argus E40 and Argus Air.

Which Voopoo Argus Snap Pod to Pick

MTL is a tight, cigarette-like draw; RDL is looser with more vapour.

  • 1.0Ω: tight MTL. Closest to a cigarette; for 20mg nic salts and first-time refillers.
  • 0.7Ω: loose MTL. Warmer and fuller; the all-rounder for 10mg salts and the pod most Argus owners run daily.
  • 0.4Ω: RDL. Airy and warm; for 30W kits and lower-strength freebase.

Voopoo Argus Snap Pod Specs

  • Capacity: 2ml. The UK TPD limit, about a day of MTL vaping.
  • Fill: clamshell top fill. A hinged cap, so no plug to lose.
  • Leak protection: Liquid-Seal Shield. Voopoo claims 30 days leak-free.
  • Airflow: on the kit, not the pod. The dial on the Voopoo Argus P3 sets the draw.

Best E-Liquid for Voopoo Argus Snap Pods

Run 50/50 e-liquid in the 1.0Ω and 0.7Ω pods, 20mg nic salts for a heavy smoker and 10mg for a lighter one; thick 70VG shortfill burns the cotton. The 0.4Ω pod takes 60/40 freebase at 3mg to 6mg. See 10ml nic salt e-liquids.

How to Fit and Prime a Voopoo Argus Snap Pod

  1. Flip the cap open, fill to below the window top, snap it shut.
  2. Let the pod stand ten minutes so the cotton soaks.
  3. Push the pod in; the magnets seat it and the chip reads the resistance.
  4. Take two short draws before a full one.

How Long Voopoo Argus Snap Pods Last

Expect one to two weeks per pod at 2ml a day, less with sweet liquids. Change it when flavour fades, tastes burnt or gurgles. A three pack covers three to six weeks, well under a pound a week.
