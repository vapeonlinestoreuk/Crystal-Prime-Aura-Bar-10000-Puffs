Best E-Liquid for Uwell Caliburn Ironfist L Pods

All three coils suit 50/50 e-liquid (equal parts PG and VG). On the 1.2Ω and 1.0Ω use 10ml nic salt e-liquids, 20mg for heavy smokers, 10mg for lighter smokers. The 0.8Ω mesh also takes 3mg to 6mg freebase 50/50. Avoid 70% VG shortfills; they burn these coils.

How to Fit and Prime an Uwell Caliburn Ironfist L Pod

  1. Pull the old pod off and push the coil out of its base.
  2. Push a fresh Uwell Caliburn G or G2 coil into the new pod until flush.
  3. Open the side plug and fill with 50/50 e-liquid, leaving an air gap.
  4. Wait 5 to 10 minutes for the cotton to soak; firing early burns the coil.
  5. Click the pod onto the device and draw gently.

How Long Uwell Caliburn Ironfist L Pods Last

The pod lasts weeks to months because the coil comes out; the coil wears first, typically 7 to 10 days. Change the pod when the mouthpiece cracks, the plug stops sealing, or it leaks after a fresh coil: about 25p a week at one every six weeks.
