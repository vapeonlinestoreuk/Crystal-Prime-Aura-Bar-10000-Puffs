Voopoo Gene Chip Modes: Smart, RBA, ECO and TC

Every Drag and Argus mod runs a Gene chip: GENE.TT on the Drag X, GENE.TT 2.0 on the Drag X2 and Drag 5, GENE TT 3.0 on the Drag 6, and the lighter Gene.AI 1.2 on the Argus P1 and P1S. The modes are the same across them. Smart reads the coil resistance and locks the wattage inside that coil's rated range, so a 0.3Ω PnP cannot be fired at 80W by mistake; most people leave it on. RBA switches the limit off and gives the full 5 to 80W (177W on a dual-cell Drag) for rebuildable decks and setting power by hand. ECO, on the Drag 4 and E60, trims output and screen brightness to stretch a charge. TC, on the Drag 4, is temperature control for Ni200, titanium and stainless coils. Turbo is listed on the Argus GT II. Pod kits such as the Vinci S and Vmate i2 run Smart mode only and set their own wattage from the pod fitted.

18650 and 21700 Cells on Drag and Argus Mods

No Voopoo mod ships with batteries, apart from the Drag 4 mod variant here that bundles two 2800mAh 25A 18650 cells for £12 more. The Drag X, X2 and X Plus take one 18650. The Drag X3 and Argus XT take one 18650 or one 21700, the larger cell with more capacity. The Drag 3, 4, 5 and Argus GT II take two 18650s for 177W to 200W. Use a cell rated at 20A or higher above 60W, charge pairs together in an external charger, and never mix cells of different age in a dual-cell mod. To skip cells altogether, the Drag S2, S3, Argus Pro 2 and Drag 6 have the battery built in.

How long do Voopoo pods last?

A fixed-coil Argus Top-Fill V2 or ITO pod lasts one to two weeks for a 10ml-a-week vaper on 50/50 nic salt, longer on a 1.0Ω or 1.2Ω pod at low power. A 0.15Ω DTL coil on sweet shortfill lasts nearer a week. Replace the pod when the flavour fades or every draw tastes dry.

How long do Voopoo coils last?

One to two weeks on a 0.6Ω to 1.2Ω PnP, TM or PnP X coil with 50/50 liquid; five to ten days on a 0.15Ω to 0.3Ω PnP X or TPP coil at 40W and above on 70VG shortfill. Prime a new coil with a few drops, fill, wait five minutes, and start at the bottom of the printed wattage range.

How do you turn on and unlock a Voopoo vape?

Button kits such as the Drag X, X2, S2 and Argus Pro 2 switch on with five quick presses of the fire button and lock or unlock with three. The Drag X3 fires by capacitive touch and the Argus XT has a side power lock switch. Draw-activated kits such as the Doric Q, Argus G2, Vmate and Vinci S have no button: fit a pod and inhale.

How do you refill a Voopoo vape?

Argus Top-Fill V2 and Vmate pods fill from the top: pull the mouthpiece off, fill the port to just below the line, refit it. Argus E40, PnP X and Drag X2 pods fill from the side through a silicone plug with the pod pulled off the device. Wait five minutes on a new pod or coil before the first draw.

Why is my Voopoo vape not working?

Check three things in order. The lock: five clicks turns a Drag on, three clicks unlocks it. The pod: pull it, wipe the magnetic contacts and reseat it, because the Gene chip will not fire on a poor connection. The coil: a PnP coil in a PnP X pod, or the reverse, will not read, and a flat 18650 in a Drag X stops output until charged.
