Which Vape Kits Suit a 50/50 Bar Salt

This 50/50 mouth-to-lung liquid belongs in a refillable pod kit or starter kit with a 1.0ohm or higher coil at low wattage; our MTL vape kits collection covers the matches. Keep it out of sub-ohm tanks and 0.4ohm pods, where a 20mg salt turns harsh and the thin mix floods the coil. For a high-wattage kit, pick a 70/30 shortfill from our e-liquid range.

Filling Your Pod and Making Each Bottle Last

  1. Shake the bottle, open the pod's fill port and fill to just under the max line.
  2. On a new pod or coil, wait five minutes before the first draw so the cotton soaks; skipping this is the main cause of a burnt first puff.
  3. Take short, cigarette-style draws rather than long lung pulls.
  • Store bottles upright, capped and out of sunlight; heat and light dull the fruit notes.
  • Swap the pod or coil when the flavour turns muted or papery, usually every one to two weeks.
  • Keep one pod per flavour, or rinse and dry it before switching, so Chocolate Spread does not linger under your next fruit.
