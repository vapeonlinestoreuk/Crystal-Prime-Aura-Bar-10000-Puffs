How to Fit a Vozol Sleek Max 10K Pod and Make It Last

  1. Pull the empty pod straight off the Vozol Sleek Max 10K battery; the magnets let it lift away without twisting.
  2. Remove the silicone plugs from the new 2ml pod's mouthpiece and base.
  3. Fit the 8ml container onto the pod as shown on the pack card, then click the pod onto the battery until the magnets seat it.
  4. Wait five minutes before the first puff so the dual mesh coil is fully wet. A dry first draw is the quickest way to a burnt pod.
  5. Take a few short draws to settle the coil, then vape as normal.

How to Make Each Pod Last Longer

  • Stay in Pure mode for everyday use; Rich mode is for flavour and empties the 10ml noticeably faster.
  • Leave a few seconds between puffs; chain-vaping a 2ml chamber outruns the refill feed and gives a thin, hot hit.
  • Store spare packs upright at room temperature with the plugs in; heat is the usual reason a stored pod weeps.
