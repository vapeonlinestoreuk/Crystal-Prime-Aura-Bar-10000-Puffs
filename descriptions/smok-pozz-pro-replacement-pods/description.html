Smok Pozz Pro replacement pods are the empty 2ml refill pods for the Smok Pozz Pro pod kit, sold as a pack of three for £3.49. Each pod takes one push-fit Smok LP1 coil (sold separately), fills from the side and clicks on magnetically. The draw is MTL: a tight, cigarette-like mouth-to-lung pull. These pods do not fit the Smok Pozz or the Smok Pozz X.

Which Kits the Smok Pozz Pro Pods Fit

They fit the Smok Pozz Pro only, the pod kit that runs on Smok LP1 coils. They do not fit these look-alike kits:

  • Smok Pozz (original): its pods have a fixed, built-in 0.8 ohm coil.
  • Smok Pozz X: it uses Smok RPM coils, not LP1.
  • Smok Novo 4 and Novo 4 Mini: they share the LP1 coil but use a different pod: buy Smok Novo 4 replacement pods for those.

There is no Smok Pozz Pro 2 or Pozz Pro Plus.

Smok Pozz Pro Pod Specs

  • Capacity: 2ml. The UK TPD version; the same pod is listed as 2.6ml outside the UK.
  • Coil: Smok LP1 series, push-fit, not included. The 0.9 ohm mesh (the kit's coil), 0.8 ohm mesh and 0.8 ohm DC MTL coils all fit.
  • Fill: side fill under a silicone stopper. Top up without touching the coil.
  • Connection: magnetic. E-liquid level visible with the pod fitted.
  • Pack size: 3 pods. About £1.16 per pod.

Best E-Liquid for Smok LP1 Coils

Use 50/50 PG/VG e-liquid or 10ml nic salts. The low-wattage 0.8 and 0.9 ohm LP1 coils cannot wick thick high-VG shortfills, which burn the cotton. Nic salts at 10mg or 20mg suit most adult smokers; 50/50 freebase at 6mg to 12mg is softer on the throat. Browse the nic salt and 50/50 e-liquid range.

How to Fit a Coil and Prime the Pod

  1. Pull the pod off the Smok Pozz Pro and push a Smok LP1 coil into the base until it stops.
  2. Drop two or three drops of e-liquid on the coil's cotton ports.
  3. Lift the side stopper, fill to just below the top and press it fully home.
  4. Click the pod back in and wait 5 to 10 minutes before the first puff.
  5. Start at the lowest wattage printed on the coil.

How Long a Smok Pozz Pro Pod Lasts

The pod outlasts the coil. Change Smok LP1 coils every one to two weeks when flavour fades, the draw gurgles or it tastes burnt; change the pod when the plastic clouds, the stopper loosens or the magnets stop holding. Not ideal for a direct-lung draw: no LP1 coil is sub-ohm. More in the Smok replacement pods range.
