How caffeine pouches work and how to use them

The caffeine in it's Caffeine Pouches passes through the lining of your gum and inner lip, the same route a nicotine pouch or caffeine snus uses, so no cup is needed.

  1. Take one pouch from the can. Do not chew, bite or swallow it.
  2. Place it between your upper lip and your gum, off to one side, and leave it there.
  3. Expect a tingle and some saliva for the first minute or two, especially on 100mg. That settles.
  4. Keep it in for as long as the tin suggests; most caffeine pouches are used for around 20 to 30 minutes, and the caffeine releases gradually.
  5. Bin the used pouch when the time is up.

50mg or 100mg: which it's Caffeine Pouches strength to pick

Filter coffee is usually put at around 90 to 100mg of caffeine, so a 100mg pouch is roughly one coffee and a 50mg pouch roughly half a coffee. If you drink tea, decaf or nothing caffeinated, start on 50mg; you can add a second pouch later, but you cannot take half of a 100mg pouch back out.

The Food Standards Agency and the European Food Safety Authority both put the adult guideline at up to 400mg of caffeine a day from all sources, and 200mg a day for pregnant women. That is official guidance, not advice from us, and it counts every coffee, tea and energy drink you have alongside the pouches.
