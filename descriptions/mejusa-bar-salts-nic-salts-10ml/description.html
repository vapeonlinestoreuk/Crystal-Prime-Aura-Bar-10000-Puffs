How to Fill, Store and Make It Last

Filling a pod with Mejusa Bar Salts Nic Salts 10ml

  1. Shake the bottle for a few seconds, then press down and turn the childproof cap to open it.
  2. Seat the thin nozzle in the pod's fill port and fill to just below the max line; no separate dripper is needed.
  3. On a new pod or coil, wait five to ten minutes before the first draw so the cotton is fully soaked, then take a few short draws to check the strength.

Make a bottle of Mejusa Bar Salts last longer

  • Store bottles upright and capped in a cool cupboard away from sunlight; heat and light dull the flavour within weeks.
  • Never pull on an empty pod; burnt cotton carries into the next fill.
  • Use a fresh pod or coil when switching between Ice and non-Ice flavours; menthol lingers in cotton for a day or more.
