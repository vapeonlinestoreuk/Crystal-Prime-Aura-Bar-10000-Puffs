Nicotine Strength Guide: How to Add a Nic Shot

Most direct-lung vapers find 3mg plenty, because a sub-ohm coil delivers far more vapour per draw than a pod; 6mg is the realistic ceiling.

  1. Unscrew the childproof cap and lift the plastic nozzle out with a thumbnail or coin edge.
  2. Add one 10ml 18mg shot for 3mg; for 6mg, pour 10ml of shortfill into a spare bottle first, then add two shots.
  3. Refit the nozzle, cap it tight and shake hard for 30 seconds until the liquid looks even.
  4. Let it stand a couple of hours; fruit blends settle within a day, so a longer steep is optional.
  5. Fill your tank, soak a new coil for ten minutes, then start at the bottom of its wattage range.

To make the bottle last, run the coil at the low end of its range, keep it capped and out of sunlight, and leave a few seconds between draws so the cotton re-wicks.
