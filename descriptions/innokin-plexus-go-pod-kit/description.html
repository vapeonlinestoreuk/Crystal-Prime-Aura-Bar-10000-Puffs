Pick Your Inhale: MTL on the Innokin Plexus Go

MTL is a tight draw held in the mouth first; RDL (restricted direct lung) is a looser pull straight to the lungs; DTL (direct to lung) is wide-open cloud vaping. This kit is MTL only, and the cartridge sets how tight it feels:

  • 1.2Ω (sold separately): tightest MTL, around 12W. Closest to a cigarette, best with 20mg nic salts, longest battery life.
  • 0.8Ω (included): standard MTL, around 15W. The everyday setting for 10mg or 20mg nic salts with the slider half open.
  • 0.6Ω (sold separately): loose MTL to light RDL, around 20W. Warmer and fuller, drains the battery fastest, suits 10mg salts or 6mg to 12mg freebase.

Innokin Plexus Go Pods and E-Liquid Pairing

Every Innokin Plexus cartridge is a mesh MTL coil, so the rule is simple: 50/50 PG/VG in 10ml bottles. Nic salts give the smoothest throat hit: 20mg for a heavy smoker, 10mg for ten a day or fewer, 5mg when stepping down. Freebase 50/50 at 6mg to 12mg suits the 0.6Ω and 0.8Ω pods. Avoid 70/30 shortfills; they wick slowly here and cause burnt pods. Browse 10ml nic salt e-liquids, most under £3.

Innokin Plexus Go Compatibility and Refills

This is the Innokin Plexus Go, not the Innokin Plexus Pro. How to Set Up the Innokin Plexus Go

  1. Remove the silicone bungs from the cartridge mouthpiece and base.
  2. Slide the top cap open, fill the 2ml cartridge with 50/50 e-liquid to just below the top, and slide it shut.
  3. Wait 10 minutes with the cartridge upright so the mesh soaks through; skip this and the first draw tastes burnt.
  4. Push the cartridge into the battery until it clicks.
  5. Set the side airflow slider, then inhale slowly for two to three seconds. The LED flashes red eight times when it needs a USB-C charge.

How to Make the Innokin Plexus Go Last Longer

  • Keep the pod above a quarter full. Running it dry kills mesh pods early.
  • Rest between draws. Chain vaping outpaces wicking and gives a hot, burnt pull.
  • Charge from a standard USB port. Innokin's manual advises against car chargers and against leaving the cell below 10% for long.
  • Wipe the contacts weekly. E-liquid creep is the usual cause of a kit that stops firing.
