## Things only Raj can fix before publishing (data, not copy)

1. **Wrong prices.** Mejusa Bar Salts £0.49 and Wow Exp 100ml £2.49 on every variant. Neither page prints a price.
2. **Wrong variant.** Voopoo Argus Snap "0.6ohm" does not exist; VOOPOO makes 0.4, 0.7 and 1.0. Rename to 0.7ohm.
3. **Duplicate listings.** Pacha Mama 50ml clashes with the ACTIVE "Charlies Chalk Dust Pacha Mama Range Shortfill 50ml" (£13.99 to £15.49): merge and 301, or retarget the old page. Ironfist L pods clash with the older draft "Empty Pod Cartridge 2.5ml 2PCS/Pack" (£5.49): archive one. LUXE X Dual Mesh pods sit beside two ACTIVE LUXE X pod pages that carry invented reviews.
4. **Missing strength options.** Mejusa and Wick Liq have no nicotine-strength option; the ranges are 5/10/20mg. Wow Exp has a "6mg" strength that two nic shots cannot produce.
5. **Compare-at prices with no prior selling history** on Vozol Sleek Max pods (£5.99) and Pacha Mama 50ml (£9.99).
6. **Variant typos**: Forst Blue (Plexus Go), Respberry / Frout (Mejusa), Peer / Lemanode / Respberry (Wick Liq), Big Belly Jelly / Jam Rock (Pacha Mama).
7. **Stock and supply checks**: Vape Club lists the Ironfist L kit as discontinued; it's Caffeine Pouches show sold out on the brand site; confirm the TFOS 0.6ohm pod is UK-notified.
8. **it's Caffeine Pouches is caffeine, not nicotine.** Do not add the "Nicotine Pouches" tag.

## Limits of this run

- The container could not open any outside website (brand sites, retailers, forums). Specs came from
  Ahrefs, web-search snippets and the VOS spec metafields. Each report marks every spec by source.
  Figures marked "retailer only" should be checked against the brand site before publishing.
- Named competitor prices were left off every page because they could not be verified live.
- No community quotes were used because no forum could be read.
- vos-onpage-audit needs a live URL, so it is deferred to publish for the fourteen drafts.
- Ahrefs used roughly 30,000 units in total (about 1,000 to 4,300 per product).
