Which Smok TFOS Pod to Pick

0.8ohm mesh: a tight MTL draw with the airflow closed in Norm mode. Best for ex-smokers and nic salt users; the kit ships with this one.

0.6ohm mesh: a warmer, looser RDL draw with more vapour, airflow open in Boost mode. Best for 50/50 freebase e-liquid.

Smok TFOS Pods Key Specs

  • Resistance: 0.8ohm mesh or 0.6ohm mesh. Higher ohms, tighter and cooler; lower ohms, warmer.
  • Capacity: 2ml (UK TPD). Around a day of moderate vaping before a top-up.
  • Fill type: side fill with a rubber stopper. No tools; the clear pod shows the level in place.
  • Coil: built-in mesh. Replace the whole pod when the coil is done.
  • Pack size: 2 pods. £6.99 a pack is £3.50 a pod.

Best E-Liquid for Smok TFOS Pods

Use 50/50 e-liquid in both pods: 10mg or 20mg nic salts in the 0.8ohm pod for a smooth MTL throat hit, 50/50 freebase at 3mg to 12mg in the 0.6ohm pod. Thick high-VG shortfills wick slowly and burn the mesh early. Stock up on 10ml nic salt e-liquids.

How to Fit and Prime a Smok TFOS Pod

  1. Pull the old pod off the kit; magnets hold it.
  2. Pull back the rubber stopper on the new pod and fill to just below the top.
  3. Push the stopper firmly closed and wipe the contacts dry.
  4. Click the pod in and wait five minutes so the mesh soaks through.
