Which Smok Novo SLR 2.0 Pod to Pick

  1. 1.0ohm mesh: tightest, coolest, cigarette-like mouth-to-lung (MTL) draw. Suits 20mg nic salts; longest pod life.
  2. 0.8ohm mesh: the all-rounder. Slightly warmer, still MTL, takes 10mg or 20mg salts. Start here if unsure.
  3. 0.6ohm mesh: warmer, looser, restricted direct-lung draw. Suits 5mg to 10mg salts or 3mg to 6mg freebase.
  4. 0.6ohm dual mesh: two mesh strips, fastest ramp, thickest vapour, quickest liquid use. Keep nicotine at 6mg or below.

Smok Novo Pod Specs

  • Capacity: 2ml per pod. The UK legal maximum; a 10ml bottle gives five fills.
  • Coil: built-in mesh, not replaceable. When the coil goes, change the whole pod.
  • Fill: top fill under the mouthpiece. Lift the rubber bung; the pod stays on the kit.

Best E-Liquid for Smok Novo Pods

Use 50/50 e-liquid or nic salts in the 0.8ohm and 1.0ohm pods; thicker 70/30 shortfill wicks too slowly and causes dry hits. Pick 10mg or 20mg from the 10ml nic salt e-liquids.

How to Fill and Prime a Smok Novo Pod

  1. Pull the mouthpiece cap straight off the pod.
  2. Lift the rubber bung and fill to just below the top line, leaving an air gap.
  3. Press the bung shut and click the mouthpiece back on.
  4. Wait at least 5 minutes so the mesh soaks before the first draw.
  5. Snap the pod onto the kit and take two or three short draws first.

How Long Smok Novo Pods Last

Smok publishes no lifespan figure. Expect roughly 1 to 2 weeks from a 0.8ohm or 1.0ohm pod and under a week from a 0.6ohm, about 15 to 20ml of liquid.
