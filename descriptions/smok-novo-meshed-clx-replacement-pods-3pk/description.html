TL;DR: Smok Novo Meshed CLX Replacement Pods are the magnetic side-fill pods for the Smok Novo Classy pod kit: three per pack at £7.49 (about £2.50 a pod), 0.6ohm meshed or 0.8ohm dual meshed, 2ml UK capacity. Not the pod for the Smok Novo 2, Novo 4, Novo 5, Novo X or Novo Axis.

Key Facts: Smok Novo Meshed CLX Replacement Pods

  • The Smok Novo Meshed CLX Replacement Pod fits the Smok Novo Classy pod kit.
  • The Smok Novo Meshed CLX Replacement Pod comes in 0.6ohm meshed and 0.8ohm dual meshed.
  • The Smok Novo Meshed CLX Replacement Pod holds 2ml in the UK version and fills from the side.
  • Smok Novo Meshed CLX Replacement Pods are sold in packs of three.

Smok Novo Classy Pods: Fits and Does Not Fit

Fits:

  • Smok Novo Classy Pod Vape Kit (its side-fill pod; the kit also takes the top-fill Smok Novo SLR pod).

Does not fit:

  • Smok Novo 2, Novo 2S, Novo 2X, Novo 2C and Novo 3, which take friction-fit Smok Novo 2 Replacement Pods.
  • Smok Novo 4, Novo 4 Mini, Novo 5, Novo X, Novo Axis and Novo Axis EZ, each with its own pod in the Smok replacement pods range.

Smok Novo CLX Pods: Pick 0.6ohm or 0.8ohm

  • 0.6ohm meshed: restricted direct-lung (RDL) or loose MTL, more vapour. Suits 50/50 at 3 to 6mg or a 10mg nic salt.
  • 0.8ohm dual meshed: tight, cigarette-like mouth-to-lung (MTL). Suits 10mg or 20mg nic salts.

Smok publishes no wattage per pod; the Smok Novo Classy reads the pod and sets its own power (5 to 35W).

Smok Novo Meshed CLX Pod Specs

  • Resistance: 0.6ohm or 0.8ohm. Lower ohms means a warmer, airier draw.
  • Coil: built-in mesh, dual mesh on the 0.8ohm. Even heat, full flavour from the first draw.
  • Capacity: 2ml (UK TPD version). Smok lists 3ml outside the UK.

Best E-Liquid for Smok Novo Classy Pods

Use 50/50 (half VG, half PG) e-liquid in both pods: 10mg or 20mg nic salts in the 0.8ohm, freebase 3 to 6mg in the 0.6ohm. Thick 70VG shortfills burn a small mesh pod. Browse 50/50 e-liquid and nic salts here.

How to Fit and Prime a Smok Novo CLX Pod

  1. Pull the old pod off the Smok Novo Classy; no twisting needed.
  2. Open the side plug and fill to just below the port, away from the chimney.
  3. Close the plug and stand the pod for five to ten minutes; Smok publishes no exact soak time.
  4. Fit the pod, wait for the screen to read it, then take two or three short draws.

How Long Smok Novo CLX Pods Last

A pod usually lasts one to two weeks; sweet liquids shorten that. Change it at a burnt taste, weak flavour or gurgling.
