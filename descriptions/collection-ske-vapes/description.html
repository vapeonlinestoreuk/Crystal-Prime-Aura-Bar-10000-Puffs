SKE Vape Questions, Answered

Are SKE vapes good?

By our order data, yes: SKE lines sold 5,085 units here in four months. The SKE Bar 600 kit holds 4.9 stars from 35 reviews, the CL6000 kit and pods 5.0 from 5 and 4, the Crystal Nic Salts 5.0 from 5. The weakest score is the 600 Pro pods at 3.8 from 5 reviews.

Can I refill a SKE vape?

Not the prefilled pods: SKE Bar, 600 Pro, CL6000, Bar 15K and 30K Pro Max pods are sealed and replaced as a unit, though the CL6000, 15K and 30K sets top up their own pod from the attached container. To refill by hand, use SKE Crystal 10ml nic salts in a refillable MTL pod kit.

How do you refill a SKE Bar 600?

You swap the pod rather than refill it. Pull the empty 2ml pod out of the top of the SKE Bar 600, take the silicone plugs off a new SKE Bar Prefilled Replacement Pod, push it in until it seats, and wait a couple of minutes before the first draw. Refill pods are £2.99 in over 35 flavours.

SKE Crystal CL6000: how do you use it?

Charge it over USB-C, remove the stickers and plugs from the pod set, click the two-piece pod into the top of the device and inhale: there is no button. The 10ml container feeds the 2ml pod as you go. When both run dry, pull the set off and click in a £5.49 SKE Crystal CL6000 replacement pod.

Which pods fit the SKE 600 Pro?

The SKE 600 Pro Prefilled Replacement Pods at £4.99, or £4.00 each at 3 for £12: 2ml, 20mg, 1.1 ohm mesh, up to 600 puffs. SKE Bar pods and Crystal Plus pods are listed for their own kits. One retailer says Crystal Plus pods also fit the 600 Pro, which we have not confirmed.

How long does a SKE bar take to charge?

Our listings give no charge time. The SKE Bar 600 and the 400mAh SKE 600 Pro charge over USB-C from a standard 5V phone charger, and review sites report about an hour for the SKE Bar. The 800mAh CL6000, 850mAh Bar 15K and 1250mAh 30K Pro Max take longer. No cable is listed in any box.

Why does my SKE vape taste burnt?

Usually a new pod fired before the cotton soaked, or a pod run dry. Fit the pod, wait two or three minutes, and take short draws for the first few puffs. On the CL6000, Bar 15K and 30K Pro Max, check the container is clicked fully home; a part-seated container starves the coil and burns the mesh.

How many puffs does a SKE vape give?

It depends on the pod: up to 600 from an SKE Bar 600 or SKE 600 Pro pod, 2,000 from the CL2000, 6,000 from the Crystal CL6000 or Bar 6K set, 15,000 from the SKE Bar 15K set and about 30,000 from the SKE 30K Pro Max twin-pod pack. All are manufacturer ratings at light draws.

Yes. UK orders go out by next day delivery, free on orders over £40. Three SKE Crystal CL6000 kits and two refill pods clear that line; a single SKE Bar 600 kit at £3.95 does not, so pair small orders with pods or nic salts to reach it.
